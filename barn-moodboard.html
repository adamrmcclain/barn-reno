Plan A

Lean & Rustic

est. $3,000 – $5,500

The barn's bones are already the design. Clean it out, seal the floor, hang string lights between the rafters, build a simple bar from reclaimed lumber, and fill it with secondhand leather and wicker. A screen panel behind the stained glass doors keeps the bugs out on summer nights while keeping the view in.

Plan B
Plan B

The Real Deal

est. $8,000 – $15,000

Everything in Plan A, done properly. An electrician runs a circuit for real pendant lighting on dimmers. The bar gets built-in cabinetry with a mini fridge and sink. A small cast iron wood stove makes it a three-season space. The floor gets refinished. The walls get cleaned, sealed, and insulated in the front room. A solid partition replaces the curtain.
