Floor Plans

Entertaining Space · Craft/Workshop · Stone Basement

A

Entertaining Space — 14 × 15.5 ft

Bar on the left wall. TV on the top wall. Sofa faces both. Double stained glass doors on the right wall open outward to the yard. Large window on the bottom wall, single window on the left and top walls.

Entertaining space — top down view

B

Craft / Workshop — 12 × 15.5 ft

With bulk storage moved to the basement, this half becomes a proper workshop and craft space. Workbench along the top wall, craft table center, tool pegboard on the left wall above the window. Stairs or access point to the basement in the back right.

Craft / workshop — top down view

What moved to the basement: Bikes, seasonal bins, sleds, lawn equipment, hoses. The upper half is now fully dedicated to active use — no shuffling bins to get to the workbench.
C

Basement — 13 × 13 ft

Stone walls, brick floor, ~7 ft ceiling. New door on the left wall (exterior access). Three windows fixed and glazed. Dehumidifier essential. Bikes on the right wall, seasonal bins along the back, lawn equipment center floor.

Basement — top down view

Check moisture after rain before committing any storage here. Stone foundations are generally excellent at drainage but need verification. A $50 dehumidifier and a moisture reading on a wet day tells you everything you need to know.
